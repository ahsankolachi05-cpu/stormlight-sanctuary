Welcome

A Space Where You Can Be Yourself

Stormlight Sanctuary offers a compassionate, individual-centred approach to mentoring and coaching.

Everyone arrives with their own story. We take time to understand your unique experiences, your needs and your way of navigating the world — then shape the support around you, never the other way round.

There is no pressure to be anyone other than who you are, and no expectation to move faster than feels comfortable. Just steady, respectful support as you find your own way forward.

  • Compassion firstYou will be met with warmth, patience and genuine respect.
  • Your pace, alwaysSessions move at a speed that feels manageable for you.
  • Room to growSpace to reflect, explore and build confidence in your own time.

Our Services

Support Shaped Around You

Three kinds of one-to-one support, each tailored to your goals, your preferences and your way of doing things. If you are unsure which fits, that is completely fine — we can work it out together.

Personal Mentoring

A steady, supportive relationship offering guidance, reflection and encouragement, tailored to your individual goals. Mentoring gives you a trusted person to think things through with, as you navigate decisions, changes and everyday challenges.

Coaching for Personal Growth

Neurodiversity & Accessibility

Support That Works With You, Not Against You

Different minds work differently — and that is something to work with, not fix. Stormlight Sanctuary is built around flexibility, clear communication and respect for individual preferences.

Flexible by design

Session length, format and pace can be adjusted. Camera on or off, breaks when you need them, agendas shared in advance — whatever helps you feel comfortable.

Clear communication

Plain language, no jargon, and written summaries after sessions if they help. You can communicate in the way that suits you best — speaking, writing, or a mix.

Your preferences lead

Tell us what you need — lighting, seating, stimming, processing time, a support person present. Your access needs are listened to and respected, without judgement.

A non-judgemental space

There is no “right” way to think, feel or communicate here. You will not be rushed, corrected or compared — you will be heard, believed and supported as you are.

About this website: this site is designed to be accessible too — clear structure, readable text, full keyboard navigation and reduced motion for anyone who prefers it. If anything here is hard to use, please tell us via the contact form and we will do our best to help another way.

About

Hello, and Welcome In

Stormlight Sanctuary was created from a simple belief: that everyone deserves a calm, respectful space to think, reflect and grow — especially when life feels stormy.

Our mentoring and coaching is personal, unhurried and centred entirely on you. We listen first, work at your pace, and help you find a way forward that genuinely fits your life and your mind.

A note for visitors: a fuller introduction from the founder — their story, background and what led them to create Stormlight Sanctuary — will be added here shortly. We would rather share the real story than a placeholder one.

Looking ahead: Stormlight Sanctuary currently offers mentoring and coaching. In time, we plan to grow into offering therapeutic counselling too — that service is not available yet, and we will share more when it is.

Our Approach

Getting Started Is Simple

Beginning something new can feel daunting. Our approach keeps things gentle, clear and free of pressure — you can stop, pause or change direction at any point.

  1. 01

    Start with a Conversation

    Reach out through the contact form below. We will reply in plain, friendly language and arrange an informal first conversation — no obligation, no sales pitch, just a chance to say hello.

  2. 02

    Explore What Matters to You

    Together, we talk through where you are now, what you would like to be different, and what support would actually help. You set the agenda; we help you make sense of it.

  3. 03

    Build a Path Forward Together

    We shape a plan around your goals, your pace and your preferences — with small, manageable steps and regular chances to reflect, adjust and notice how far you have come.

There is no commitment until you feel ready — and no promise of quick fixes, just steady support along the way.

Accessibility Statement

Accessibility matters deeply to Stormlight Sanctuary. This website is designed with WCAG 2.2 AA principles in mind, including:

  • Strong colour contrast and text that never relies on colour alone
  • Full keyboard navigation with clearly visible focus states
  • Clear headings, labels and descriptive links
  • Large, comfortable clickable targets and generous spacing
  • Motion that is gentle, and removed entirely if you prefer reduced motion
